About me

I'm a clinical psychologist. I studied psychology at the University of Exeter and clinical psychology at Vilnius University. I have lived in several countries and now live in Berlin.

For as long as I can remember, I have been curious about people: what we feel and why we act the way we do. That curiosity led me to study psychology, and it is still with me.

How I work

I believe there is no single right way to live. Everyone experiences life in their own way and comes to consultations with their own unique questions. It matters to me to get to know each person deeply and help them make sense of their thoughts and feelings, so they can find their own answers.

Research shows that the relationship between psychologist and client is one of the things that helps most in consultations. So I don't rush, and I take time to create a safe space between us. I have the most experience working with anxiety, ADHD, burnout and mood swings. I am an LGBTQ+ affirming psychologist.
