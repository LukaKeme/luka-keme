What people often come to me with

Experiences of moving between cultures and dealing with changes and emotions coming from them. Living with anxiety you feel in your body or in thoughts that won't settle. Experiencing emotions intensely and feeling that they change quickly. Difficulties with attention or impulsivity, with an ADHD diagnosis or recognising yourself in its symptoms. Searching for your own way and trying to understand who you are and how you would like to live. Something you can't put into words yet, which is where many people start.

If several fit, or none do, you can start with the free call. During these 20 minutes we will try to clarify a direction and question to work on in consultations. You can also use this time to see whether we are a good fit.

I'm Luka

I studied psychology in England and Lithuania and now live in Berlin. I know what moving and starting over abroad is like: how enriching it can be, and how hard it can get.

I'm training in existential-humanistic psychotherapy. In this approach, what matters most is understanding how you experience your own life, rather than trying to change your personality.

Alongside consultations, I research emotion regulation in bipolar disorder with the ENBER network. I like to spend my free time hiking, travelling and creating. My creativity takes shape in photography, painting and DJing.
