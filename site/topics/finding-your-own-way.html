How it can feel

Sometimes life looks fine from the outside, and still something doesn't fit. The job, the city, the relationship, or the plans you made years ago.

Questions like who am I, what do I actually want and what matters to me can be unsettling. They can also be the start of a life that feels more like your own.

This is close to how I work. In the existential-humanistic approach there are no right answers handed to you from outside. We look at your values, your choices and your fears, so you can find answers that are yours.

What people often find hardest

  • Big decisions

    Changing careers, moving, ending or starting a relationship. Before you decide, we can look at what pulls you in each direction.

  • Living by other people's expectations

    Family, culture or your own old plans can shape a life that no longer fits. We can explore what you would choose for yourself.

  • Identity

    Who you are can change over time, including your sexuality or gender identity. I am an LGBTQ+ affirming psychologist, and you can explore this at your own pace.

  • Meaning

    Feeling empty or stuck even when things are going well. We can look at what gives your days weight, and what is missing.
