How it can feel

Many adults with ADHD have spent years hearing that they are lazy, too much, or not trying hard enough. Often they believed it.

ADHD can mean losing focus on boring tasks and losing track of time on interesting ones, starting many things and finishing few, forgetting appointments, acting before thinking, or feeling restless inside. It can also bring curiosity, creativity and a lot of warmth.

ADHD is one of the topics I have the most experience with. In consultations we look at what is hard for you right now, and at what already works.

What adults with ADHD often find hardest

  • Getting started and finishing

    Knowing what to do is rarely the problem. We can look at what gets in the way and find structures that suit your brain.

  • Time

    Being late, missing deadlines, underestimating how long things take. Small practical changes can make a big difference.

  • Emotions

    Many adults with ADHD feel emotions intensely and react quickly. This is common, and it can be worked with.

  • Shame and self-criticism

    Years of feeling different leave a mark. We can look at the story you tell about yourself, and make room for a kinder one.

If you would like to meet other adults with ADHD, have a look at the online group for adults with ADHD.
