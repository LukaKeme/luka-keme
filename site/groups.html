Group for adults with ADHD

Registration is open
Register, English groupRegistracija, lietuviška grupė

For adults with an ADHD diagnosis, and for those still wondering whether it fits them. It can also help if you live close to someone with ADHD and want to understand better how the ADHD brain works. The English and Lithuanian groups follow the same six-topic programme.

Each session has a 30-minute talk followed by an hour of discussion.

  1. Week 1
    Understanding the ADHD brain

    What research has established about ADHD, what a diagnosis involves, and which claims are only noise.

  2. Week 2
    Getting started

    Starting tasks, managing time and finishing what you begin, for when knowing what to do is not the problem.

  3. Week 3
    Emotions

    What research says about emotional intensity, mood changes and emotion regulation in adults with ADHD.

  4. Week 4
    Me in relation to others

    Relationships, masking, and choosing when and with whom to open up.

  5. Week 5
    Self-acceptance

    How to live a good life with the brain you have, rather than trying to change yourself.

  6. Week 6
    What managed ADHD looks like

    What life with well-managed ADHD looks like, and how to look after your wellbeing in the long term.
