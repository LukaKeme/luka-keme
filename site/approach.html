My approach

The existential-humanistic approach

This approach helps you understand your life more deeply, and how you live it.

It sees each person as inseparable from their life circumstances and relationships. Together we look at the patterns and dilemmas that repeat in your life, and at how you could build a future that feels meaningful to you. We also make room for questions everyone meets sooner or later, such as freedom and responsibility or the search for meaning.

What I like most about existential-humanistic psychotherapy is meeting each client as an equal, and being able to see each person's life through their eyes, without one template for everyone.

I am an LGBTQ+ affirming psychologist.

Training and supervision

I'm training in existential-humanistic psychotherapy at the Institute of Humanistic and Existential Psychotherapy (HEPI), a European-accredited psychotherapy training institute. I work under the supervision of an experienced existential-humanistic psychotherapist.

My research on emotion regulation with ENBER keeps me up to date with new scientific findings, and so does updating the curriculum for my ADHD and stress groups. It helps me stay critical and keep looking for more knowledge.

I offer psychological consultations online, in English or Lithuanian.

When I may not be the right person

I may not be the right person if you are looking for crisis intervention, a formal diagnosis, medication or changes to your medication treatment, or very structured short-term consultations. In those cases, I could only refer you to a more suitable specialist.
